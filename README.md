# Adventure Game: The Fellowship of the Ring

A text-based adventure game based on J.R.R. Tolkien's _The Lord of the Rings_. Choose to play as Gandalf, Frodo, or Gollum and make decisions that affect the fate of Middle-earth.

## Gameplay

Embark on an epic journey where your choices determine the outcome. Will you lead the Fellowship to Mount Doom, succumb to the power of the One Ring, or reclaim your "precious"?

## Features

- Multiple playable characters: Gandalf, Frodo, or Gollum.
- Branching storylines with various endings.
- Randomized events for unique playthroughs.

## Requirements

- C++11 or higher compiler.

## Compilation

To compile the game, navigate to the directory containing `main.cpp` and run:

```bash
g++ -std=c++11 -o adventure_game main.cpp
```
